At connection Established state mein aa jata hai aur data transfer shuru hojata hai.TCP Connection Terminal (Four-way Handshake)Connection close karne ke liye 4 steps lagte hain kyunki TCP Full duplex hai (dono sides se alag alag close karna padta hai).Step-1: FIN (Finish) \(\rightarrow \) Jab client ka data khatam ho jata hai woh ek FIN segment bhejta hai. Iska matlab "Maine apna data bhej diya, mein close kar raha hu".Step-2: ACK \(\rightarrow \) Server is FIN ko acknowledge karta hai. Ab client-to-server line close ho gayi hai, lekin server abhi bhi data bhej sakta hai (Ise half-close kehte hain).Step-3: FIN \(\rightarrow \) Jab server ka bhi kaam ho jata hai, toh woh bhi FIN bhejta hai.Step-4: ACK \(\rightarrow \) Client last ack bhejta hai. Kuch der wait karne ke baad (Time-wait state) connection puri tarah se terminate ho jata hai.
