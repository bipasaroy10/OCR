HLEN (Header Length):Yeh 4 bit ka field hota hai.Logic: \(\text{Header Size} = \text{HLEN} \times 4 \text{ bytes}\)Agar \(\text{HLEN}\) value \(5\) hai toh \(\rightarrow 5 \times 4 = 20 \text{ bytes (min size)}\)Agar \(\text{HLEN}\) value \(15\) (\(F\)) hai, toh \(\rightarrow 15 \times 4 = 60 \text{ bytes (max size)}\)⑤ Numerical Rules (Window Sizes)Sequence no. agar '\(m\)'-bits ka hai:GBN (Go-Back-N)\(\text{Sender window size (max)} = 2^m - 1\)\(\text{Receiver window} = 1 \text{ hota hai.}\)Selective Repeat\(\text{Same for both (size)} = 2^{m-1}\)⑥ SCTP (Stream Control Transmission Protocol)UDP and TCP ka mixture hai.Multihoming \(\rightarrow \) Ek hi connection ke liye multiple IP addresses use kar sakta hai.Multi-streaming \(\rightarrow \) Ek hi connection alag alag streams bhej sakta hai bina ek dusre ko block kiye.Association \(\rightarrow \) Isme connection ko "association" kehte hain.SCTP ACK \(\rightarrow \) Isme batata hai ki kaunsa data miss hua hai.Ex:- Using WiFi and 5G. One stops then other will automatically starts without disturbing the connection.
