2 Why use Port numbers instead of Process IDs . ? (PIDs)

• Portability -> Process IDs change every time an app restarts,
but well known ports (like 80 for HTTP) stay
the same .

• Diff OS -> Windows ka PID format alag ho sakta hai, linux
ka alag. Port no. duniya mein universal standard
hain.

3 TCP Congestion Control (Traffic Management)

• Slow start -> At first, TCP darta hai, isliye CWND = 1
segment se shuru karta hai. aur RTT (Round
Trip Time) par usse double karta hai (2, 4, 8, 16...)

• Congestion avoidance -> Jab ek limit (threshold) aa jata hai, toh
ye darne lagta hai aur speed slow kar deta
hai. Ab ye sirf +1 badhata hai. (17, 18, 19)...

• Congestion Detection -> Agar koi packet loss hua, toh TCP ko lagta hai
"Traffic jam ho gaya", woh apni window
size ko aadha (half) kar deta hai taaki
network par load kam ho jaye.
